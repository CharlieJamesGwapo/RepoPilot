# Responsive interfaces

## Purpose

Adapt content and interactions to available space without losing task completion.

## Workflow

1. Start from the content and its minimum usable space rather than device brand names.
2. Identify what must remain visible and which secondary regions may reorganize.
3. Prefer flexible sizing and content-driven wrapping over fixed viewport assumptions.
4. Choose breakpoints where the layout actually stops working and record the reason.
5. Provide usable navigation and actions for touch, keyboard, and pointer input.
6. Test narrow, intermediate, and wide widths plus portrait and landscape arrangements.
7. Check long content, validation errors, empty lists, and expanded panels at each size.
8. Inspect horizontal overflow and intentional scroll regions without trapping interaction.
9. Check text zoom and reflow; preserve reading order when columns rearrange.
10. Render the final result in available browsers and report untested environments honestly.

## Deliverable

Responsive rules and evidence of complete tasks at representative viewport and content sizes.

## Evidence

Essential information and controls remain available without unintended clipping or page-wide overflow.

Record the relevant inputs, observed results, and limits of the check.
Do not substitute confidence, a screenshot alone, or a prior run for current evidence.
