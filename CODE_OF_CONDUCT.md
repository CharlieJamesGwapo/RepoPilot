# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions, explain disagreements constructively, respect privacy, and focus criticism on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply decisions consistently and explain them privately when appropriate. Report concerns through the project's published private maintainer contact once established. Do not publish personal information in an issue. A private reporting contact must be established before opening a public community.
